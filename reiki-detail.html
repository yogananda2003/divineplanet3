🌺 What is Reiki?

Reiki is an ancient healing practice that originated in India thousands of years ago and was later revived in Japan by **Dr. Mikao Usui**.

The term Reiki comes from two Japanese words:

Rei

Universal or Divine Consciousness

Ki

Life Force Energy

Together, they describe the universal life energy that flows through all living things. When this energy flows freely, we experience harmony, strength, and clarity. When blocked, we may feel stress, illness, or imbalance. Through gentle hand placements or energy channeling, Reiki restores this natural flow, encouraging physical healing, emotional peace, and spiritual growth. It is safe, non-invasive, and deeply relaxing, making it suitable for everyone.

✨ How Reiki Works

Reiki works by channeling universal life energy through the practitioner to the recipient. This energy flows to where it is most needed, clearing blockages and restoring balance.

The human body contains seven major energy centers, or **chakras**, that regulate the flow of Ki energy. When the chakras are balanced, energy circulates freely, supporting health and harmony. When they are blocked, it can manifest as stress, fatigue, or physical illness.

A Reiki session helps to cleanse and realign these chakras, activating the body’s own **natural healing ability**. As Reiki energy flows, one may feel warmth, tingling, or a deep sense of peace — signs that healing energy is working.

Regular Reiki sessions can help:

  • Reduce stress and anxiety
  • Strengthen the immune system
  • Encourage emotional balance
  • Support physical and spiritual healing
  • Restore harmony to body, mind, and spirit
